Sample checklist questions

Authentication and Account Protection Checklist

  • Is MFA enabled on your primary email account?
  • Have you enabled passkeys for important online accounts that support them?
  • Do you know how to verify whether a website is legitimate before entering personal information or payment details?
  • If your mobile phone unexpectedly loses service, do you know that this could indicate an unauthorised SIM replacement or number transfer and that you should contact your mobile provider promptly?
  • Would you independently verify an investment, product or service even if it appears to be endorsed in a convincing video or audio recording by a well-known or trusted person?

Safe Banking, Shopping & Financial Scam Protection Checklist

  • Do you regularly review your bank and credit card transactions?
  • Do you independently verify new or changed bank account details before transferring money?
  • Would you independently verify claims that a well-known person, financial expert or trusted organisation endorses an investment?

Personal Backup & Recovery Checklist

  • Do you back up important files regularly?
  • Have you tested restoring files from backup?

Practical Toolkit

Protect your accounts, devices, and personal information

The Personal Cyber Safety Toolkit is designed for people who want to improve their cyber safety but are unsure where to begin. It provides practical, step-by-step guidance to help you review how you currently protect your accounts, devices, personal information and finances, and identify realistic improvements.

The toolkit focuses on everyday cyber risks including compromised accounts, weak authentication, account recovery gaps, unsecured devices, scams and phishing, financial and investment fraud, data loss, and inadequate backup and recovery arrangements.

It is suitable for individuals, families, students, retirees, mobile phone users, and anyone who wants a structured and practical way to strengthen their personal cyber resilience.
