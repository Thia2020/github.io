MAINFRAME MODERNISATION

Mainframe-to-Cloud Security Considerations

A professional portfolio page exploring identity transformation, privileged access, operational resilience, and governance considerations when modernising mission-critical legacy platforms.

OVERVIEW

Modernising critical platforms

Mainframe environments continue to support mission-critical services across financial services, insurance, government, and other regulated sectors. Modernisation programs often involve integrating or migrating these platforms into cloud ecosystems while maintaining security, availability, regulatory compliance, and operational resilience.

This page draws upon enterprise delivery experience and presents a governance-focused view of the security considerations involved in large-scale transformation initiatives.

SECURITY CONSIDERATIONS

Critical areas requiring attention

Identity Transformation

Mapping RACF and ACF2 identities into modern identity governance processes while maintaining least privilege and segregation of duties, and auditability.

Privileged Access

Managing elevated access across hybrid environments so administrative activities remain controlled, monitored, and auditable.

Operational Resilience

Protecting critical services through disaster recovery planning, backup validation, failover testing, monitoring, and secure operational transition.

DELIVERY APPROACH

Bridging mainframe and modern technology

Successful modernisation requires collaboration across infrastructure, security, application teams, cloud engineering, vendors, and business stakeholders. Security and resilience controls should be considered throughout the project lifecycle, not introduced only during final implementation.
