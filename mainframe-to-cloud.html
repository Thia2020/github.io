MAINFRAME MODERNISATION

Mainframe-to-Cloud Security Considerations

A professional portfolio page exploring identity transformation, privileged access, operational resilience, monitoring, and governance challenges when modernising mission-critical legacy platforms.

OVERVIEW

Modernising critical legacy platforms

Mainframe environments continue to support many of the world's largest financial institutions, insurers, and government organisations. Modernisation programs often involve integrating or migrating these platforms into cloud ecosystems while maintaining security, availability, regulatory compliance, and operational resilience.

This page draws upon enterprise delivery experience and presents a governance-focused view of the security considerations involved in large-scale transformation initiatives.

SECURITY CONSIDERATIONS

Critical areas requiring attention

Identity Transformation

Migrating RACF, ACF2, or Top Secret identities into modern identity governance platforms while maintaining least privilege and segregation of duties.

Privileged Access

Managing elevated access across hybrid environments and ensuring administrative activities remain controlled, monitored, and auditable.

Operational Resilience

Protecting critical services through disaster recovery planning, backup validation, failover testing, and secure operational transition.

DELIVERY APPROACH

Bridging legacy and modern technology

Successful modernisation requires collaboration across infrastructure, security, application teams, cloud engineering, vendors, and business stakeholders. Security controls should be considered throughout the project lifecycle rather than being introduced only during final implementation stages.
