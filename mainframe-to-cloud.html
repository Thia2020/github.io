MAINFRAME REPLATFORMING

Mainframe-to-Cloud Security Considerations

A portfolio page demonstrating how identity, privileged access management, operational resilience, disaster recovery, governance, and cybersecurity controls can be integrated into enterprise mainframe modernisation and cloud migration initiatives.

OVERVIEW

Migrating critical applications

Mainframe environments continue to support mission-critical services across financial services, insurance, government, and other regulated sectors. Replatforming programs often involve migrating applications and large volumes of sensitive business data into cloud ecosystems while maintaining security, data integrity, availability, regulatory compliance, and operational resilience.

This page draws upon actual enterprise delivery experience and presents a governance-focused view of the security considerations involved in large-scale transformation initiatives.

SECURITY CONSIDERATIONS

Critical areas requiring attention

Identity Transformation

Mapping RACF and ACF2 identities into modern identity governance processes while maintaining least privilege and segregation of duties, and auditability.

Privileged Access

Managing elevated access across hybrid environments so administrative activities remain controlled, monitored, and auditable.

Operational Resilience

Protecting critical services through disaster recovery planning, backup strategy, failover capability, monitoring, and secure operational transition.

Data Migration

Protecting data integrity and confidentiality during large-scale migration through secure extraction, validation, reconciliation, and controlled cutover process.

DELIVERY APPROACH

Bridging mainframe and cloud technology

Successful migration requires collaboration across infrastructure, security, application teams, data specialists, cloud engineering, vendors, and business stakeholders. Security, data integrity, and operation resilience controls should be considered throughout the project lifecycle, not introduced only during final implementation.
