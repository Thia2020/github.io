Supporting Emerging IT Talent at CDU Sydney IT CodeFair 2026

By Cynthia Wilkinson | Thia-W Computing Solutions  •  15 November 2026

On 11 November 2026, I had the pleasure of serving as an industry judge at Charles Darwin University's Sydney IT CodeFair, evaluating student projects across the Coding, Research, Poster and AI Challenge competitions.

I also participated in the Employer Speed Dating session, where I spoke with students about their interests and career aspirations.

It was a full day, but a rewarding one. I was impressed by the creativity, technical capabilities and thought that went into the students' work. It was particularly interesting to hear students explain the problems they were trying to solve and the decisions behind their projects.

My thanks to Dr Jamal El-Den and Charles Darwin University for the invitation and the opportunity to contribute. It was wonderful connecting with Dr Pritika, Bharanidharan Shanmugam, Angelica Veness and Ismail Feroz Mohammed, and getting to know some of the talented students behind the projects.

Congratulations to all participants and everyone involved in making the event a success.

Acknowledgement from CDU Sydney

Following the event, Dr Jamal El-Den, Sydney IT CodeFair Chairperson, shared the following message of appreciation with all participating industry judges:

“Me, the IT staff and the students at the IT Discipline in Sydney would like to extend our deepest appreciation for your contributions as judges from the IT industries in Sydney.

We, the staff at CDU Sydney, were honored to have you with us judging our students' work in the different competitions.

Thank you all very much for being the most important contributors to our success.”

Dr Jamal El-Den Senior Lecturer, Information Technology Sydney IT CodeFair Chairperson Charles Darwin University

This message was addressed to all industry judges at the 2026 CDU Sydney IT CodeFair. I was pleased to be among those who contributed to the event.

Cynthia Wilkinson is the Director & Principal Cybersecurity Consultant at Thia-W Computing Solutions.
