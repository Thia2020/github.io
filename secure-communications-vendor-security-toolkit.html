Example questions from the toolkit

Secure Communications Checklist

  • Is MFA or phishing-resistant authentication enabled for business email accounts?
  • Are unusual or high-risk requests received by email independently verified before action is taken?
  • Are requests to change vendor, customer or other bank account details independently verified using trusted contact information already held by the organisation?
  • Do staff know how to independently verify unusual communications that appear to come from vendors?
  • Have alternative communication arrangements been tested during a realistic disruption scenario?

Vendor Security Assessment Checklist

  • Does the vendor access, process, store or transmit sensitive, confidential or personal information?
  • Does the vendor use MFA or other strong authentication for access to relevant systems and services?
  • Are requests to change bank account or payment details independently verified using trusted contact information already held by the organisation?
  • Is the vendor expected to notify your organisation of a cyber or security incident that may affect your information, systems or services?
  • Has your organisation considered an alternative vendor, workaround or contingency arrangement if the vendor becomes unavailable?

These are sample questions from the toolkit. The full checklists provide a structured assessment across communication security, verification, vendor access, security controls, incident management, data protection, governance and operational resilience.
