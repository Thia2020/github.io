Secure Communications & Vendor Security Toolkit

A practical toolkit designed to help small businesses and community organisations improve communication security, assess supplier risk, maintain trusted contact records, and prepare for communication-related incidents.

Overview

Why secure communications and vendor security matter

Business email compromise, invoice redirection, impersonation, insecure messaging, and supplier-related incidents can cause financial loss, operational disruption, and reputational damage. This toolkit helps organisations take practical steps to protect trusted communications and improve supplier security oversight.

Preview

Example questions from the toolkit

Secure Communications Checklist

  • Do you verify payment changes using a trusted contact method?
  • Do staff know how to recognise impersonation or invoice redirection attempts?
  • Are sensitive files shared using approved and secure methods?
  • Do you use multi-factor authentication on email and business communication platforms?

Vendor Security Assessment Checklist

  • Do vendors protect access to your systems or data using strong authentication?
  • Do vendors notify you promptly if a security incident affects your services or data?
  • Do you know which suppliers handle sensitive, financial, or personal information?
  • Are critical suppliers reviewed before onboarding and periodically after engagement?
