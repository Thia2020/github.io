Our Approach

Cyber resilience has to work in the real world.

Many very small businesses do not have separate security, technology, risk and management teams. The business owner may also be the person doing the work, paying suppliers, communicating with customers and making technology decisions.

Understand the business

Cyber resilience starts with understanding what the business depends on, what matters most and how it actually operates.

Analyse relevant threats

Not every cyber threat carries the same significance for every business. The focus is on realistic threats and risks that could affect operations, finances, information or reputation.

Prioritise practical action

Recommendations should reflect the organisation's size, resources, priorities and operating environment.

Prepare to recover

Cyber resilience is not only about prevention. It is also about being able to respond, recover and continue operating when something goes wrong.
