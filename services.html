Cyber Threat and Risk Assessment

Understand what matters to your business and what could disrupt it.

Threat analysis starts with your important assets and considers how they could be targeted, compromised or disrupted. From there, risks can be identified and practical controls prioritised.

What the assessment considers

Depending on your organisation's needs, this may include:

  • Important accounts, systems, information and business assets
  • Relevant cyber threat scenarios
  • Existing safeguards and controls
  • Resilience gaps and weaknesses
  • Potential operational and business impacts
  • Supplier, vendor and communications dependencies where relevant

What you gain

The goal is to provide a clearer picture of your current cyber risk and resilience position so that decisions about improvements are based on what matters most to your business.

Outcome: Greater visibility of priority risks, existing strengths and areas requiring attention.

Cyber Resilience Improvement Plan

Turn assessment findings into practical priorities.

Once key risks and gaps are understood, findings are translated into practical actions that are prioritised according to your organisation's risks, operating environment and available resources.

Improvement priorities may include

  • Account, identity and access protection
  • Backup and recovery preparedness
  • Incident response and escalation arrangements
  • Secure communications and fraud prevention
  • Vendor and supplier risk
  • Policies, procedures and staff practices

A practical roadmap

The aim is not to produce a long list of undifferentiated recommendations. The improvement plan identifies what should be addressed first, what can follow and why those priorities matter.

Outcome: A practical roadmap for improving cyber resilience over time.

Implementation & Technical Coordination

Move priority improvements into practical action.

An assessment creates value when appropriate improvements are implemented. Thia-W can support agreed actions and coordinate specialist cybersecurity expertise where deeper technical work is required.

Implementation support

Support can include clarifying priorities, documenting requirements, coordinating agreed actions and reviewing progress against the improvement plan.

Specialist expertise where needed

Some improvements may require deeper technical expertise. Where that is the case, Thia-W can help define the requirement and coordinate the appropriate specialist support.

Outcome: Priority improvements progress from recommendation into practical implementation.

Cyber Resilience Review

Review, learn and keep improving.

Cyber resilience changes as your organisation, technology, suppliers and threat environment change. A review helps reassess your position and identify what should be improved next.

A review may consider

  • Changes to cyber risks and threat scenarios
  • Progress against agreed improvement actions
  • Changes to systems, suppliers or operating practices
  • Lessons learned from incidents or near misses
  • New resilience priorities

Cyber resilience is ongoing

Resilience is not achieved through a single assessment or control. It requires continued visibility, learning and adaptation as the organisation and its environment change.

Outcome: An updated view of your resilience position and the priorities that should come next.

The approach is adapted to the size, risks and operating reality of each business.
