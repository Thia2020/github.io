Overview

Why this toolkit was created

Many individuals and organisations recognise the importance of cybersecurity but lack dedicated resources or are unsure where to begin. This toolkit was developed to provide practical, achievable measures that strengthen resilience, improve preparedness, and support informed decision-making.

Practical Toolkit

Cyber resilience tools and checklists

Small businesses and community organisations often know cybersecurity is important but struggle to know where to begin. The Cyber Resilience Toolkit brings together practical resources developed from enterprise delivery experience and adapted to support individuals, small businesses, not-for-profit organisations, and community groups seeking sensible, business-focused guidance.

Designed to improve readiness without unnecessary complexity, the toolkit provides ready-to-use templates and checklists covering cyber health assessments, risk management, incident response, identity protection, backup readiness, vendor security, and executive reporting.

Developed from enterprise delivery experience and adapted for smaller organisations, these templates are designed to help individuals and organisations take practical steps towards stronger cyber resilience.
