Cyber Resilience Toolkit

Cyber Resilience Toolkit for Small Business

A practical cybersecurity toolkit designed to help small businesses improve cyber resilience through straightforward governance, identity protection, backup planning, incident readiness, and business-focused security practices.

Overview

Why this toolkit was created

Many small businesses do not have dedicated cybersecurity staff but still face significant risks from phishing, ransomware, credential theft, and accidental data loss. This toolkit was developed to provide practical, low-cost measures that improve operational resilience and reduce cyber risk.

Toolkit Contents

Included resources

Cyber Health Check

A simple assessment covering identity protection, Microsoft 365 security, endpoint protection, backup arrangements, and access controls.

Incident Response Template

A practical step-by-step guide to help small businesses respond to phishing attacks, business email compromise, and ransomware events.

Backup & Recovery Checklist

A structured checklist for validating backups, testing recovery procedures, and improving business continuity readiness.

Supporting Materials

Governance and risk tools

The toolkit also includes practical resources such as vendor risk checklists, executive cyber summaries, basic risk registers, and guidance for communicating technical risks to business owners.

Practical Toolkit

Cyber resilience tools and checklists

Small businesses and community organisations often know cybersecurity is important but struggle to know where to begin. The Cyber Resilience Toolkit brings together practical resources developed from enterprise delivery experience and adapted to support smaller organisations seeking sensible, business-focused guidance.

Designed to improve readiness without unnecessary complexity, the toolkit provides ready-to-use templates and checklists covering cyber health assessments, risk management, incident response, identity protection, backup readiness, vendor security, and executive reporting.

Developed from enterprise delivery experience and adapted for smaller organisations, these templates are designed to help businesses take practical steps towards stronger cyber resilience.

What's included

  • Cyber Health Check
  • Cyber Risk Register Template
  • Incident Response Card
  • Backup & Recovery Checklist
  • Identity Protection Checklist
  • Vendor Security Assessment Checklist
  • Executive Cyber Summary Template
