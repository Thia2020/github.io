Small Business Cyber Resilience Toolkit

A practical toolkit designed to help small businesses and community organisations identify cyber risks, improve preparedness, strengthen resilience, and respond confidently to cybersecurity incidents.

Overview

Why this toolkit was created

Small businesses often face the same cyber threats as larger organisations but without dedicated cybersecurity staff or significant budgets. This toolkit was developed to provide practical, achievable guidance that helps organisations improve cyber resilience using straightforward templates, checklists, and action plans.

Who It's For

Designed for organisations

Small Businesses

Identify cyber risks, strengthen resilience, and improve preparedness using practical templates and checklists.

Community Organisations

Protect information, improve governance, and reduce cyber risk with limited resources.

Business Leaders

Gain visibility of cyber risks and develop practical action plans to improve organisational resilience.

Preview

Sample cyber health check questions

  • Do you use multi-factor authentication for business accounts?
  • Are critical business systems backed up regularly?
  • Have you identified your most important business information assets?
  • Is there a documented process for responding to cyber incidents?
  • Have staff received cyber security awareness guidance?

Practical Toolkit

Cyber resilience tools and checklists

Small businesses and community organisations often know cybersecurity is important but struggle to know where to begin. The Small Business Cyber Resilience Toolkit brings together practical resources developed from enterprise delivery experience and adapted for smaller organisations seeking practical, business-focused guidance.

Designed to improve readiness without unnecessary complexity, the toolkit provides ready-to-use templates and checklists covering cyber health assessments, risk management, incident response, identity protection, backup readiness, vendor security, and executive reporting.

Developed from enterprise delivery experience and adapted for smaller organisations, these templates are designed to help individuals and organisations take practical steps towards stronger cyber resilience.
