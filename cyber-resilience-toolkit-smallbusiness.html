Sample checklist questions

Business Identity Protection Checklist

  • Is Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) enabled for business email accounts?
  • Are user permissions reviewed regularly and updated when responsibilities change?
  • Are user accounts disabled or removed promptly when staff leave the organisation?
  • Are staff encouraged to verify unusual requests using an independent communication channel?
  • Are business mobile devices protected by PINs, passcodes, biometrics or other supported authentication methods?

Business Backup and Recovery Checklist

  • Has the organisation determined how quickly critical systems, information and business processes need to be recovered following a disruption?
  • Is at least one backup copy offline, immutable or otherwise isolated from production systems to help protect it from ransomware and accidental deletion?
  • Have recovery procedures been tested through realistic recovery exercises?
